<section class="section-block" id="features">
	<div class="section-heading">
		<p class="eyebrow">Core surfaces</p>
		<h2>A compact coaching command center.</h2>
		<p>The mobile app is organized around the same things cyclists actually check after a ride: what happened, how hard it really was, and what to do next.</p>
	</div>

	<div class="feature-grid">
		<article class="feature-card">
			<p class="feature-kicker">Dashboard</p>
			<h3>Weekly snapshot with coaching direction</h3>
			<p>See whether your last 7 days are moving you forward, with a next-ride recommendation. With enough ride history, an 8-week training analysis surfaces patterns in your endurance build, intensity balance, and recovery — with clear strengths, risks, and a suggested focus.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="feature-card">
			<p class="feature-kicker">Rides</p>
			<h3>Ride-by-ride analysis that goes beyond averages</h3>
			<p>Every imported ride gets a classification, heart-rate and power zone breakdown, Normalized Power, Intensity Factor, TSS, cadence trends, and a coaching note in plain language. A training-trends view tracks load, distance, and zone mix across your history. On iOS, tap any ride to see a zone-coloured GPS map with a fatigue signature graph.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="feature-card">
			<p class="feature-kicker">Claim new ground</p>
			<h3>Your riding, on the map</h3>
			<p>Every ride claims the tiles it crossed. Watch your map fill in over real roads and terrain, and let the blank patches next door pick your next route. Free, with no account — and it backfills every ride you have already imported.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="feature-card">
			<p class="feature-kicker">Goals</p>
			<h3>Event readiness tied to your actual training</h3>
			<p>Set a target event with distance, elevation, and date. Chad calculates readiness from your recent long rides and climbing work, counts down the days, and shapes its recommendations around the time you have left.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="feature-card">
			<p class="feature-kicker">Unlock</p>
			<h3>One-time purchase for deeper coaching</h3>
			<p>Unlock complete ride history, full fitness trend with fatigue and form curves, all-time power curve from 1 second to 3 hours, structured training plans with Today's Workout exportable as a FIT file, and more — all with a single one-time purchase. No subscription.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="feature-card">
			<p class="feature-kicker">Import</p>
			<h3>Start from everything you have</h3>
			<p>Import single rides from Garmin, Wahoo, Karoo, or anything that exports FIT, TCX, or GPX — or export your Strava archive and bring years of riding across in one go. Single rides can import in the background while you keep using the app.</p>
		</article>
	</div>
</section>

<section class="section-block" id="privacy">
	<div class="section-heading">
		<p class="eyebrow">Privacy-first</p>
		<h2>Your ride data stays close to you.</h2>
		<p>The product promise is simple: clear boundaries, local storage, and no ad-tech hiding behind "insights." The full public policy is part of this page so the privacy model stays visible without a second click.</p>
	</div>

	<div class="trust-grid">
		<article class="trust-card">
			<h3>Ride processing stays on-device</h3>
			<p>Ride data imported from FIT, TCX, and GPX files is processed entirely on-device and stored in a local database on your phone. The map is drawn from your rides on-device too.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="trust-card">
			<h3>No ads and no analytics SDKs</h3>
			<p>Chad does not use advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics SDKs to profile your training behavior.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="trust-card">
			<h3>No account required</h3>
			<p>You can import rides and use Chad without creating an account or sending your ride library to a cloud profile.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="trust-card">
			<h3>Backup and restore, on your terms</h3>
			<p>Built-in backup and restore keeps you in control — your data is yours to keep, move, or delete. It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="trust-card">
			<h3>Purchases stay in the purchase flow</h3>
			<p>In-app purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous receipt to validate the unlock, not your ride history.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="trust-card">
			<h3>Private by default</h3>
			<p><strong>Public policy summary:</strong> Chad does not upload imported ride data, and your coaching history stays on your device by default.</p>
		</article>
	</div>
</section>

	<div class="section-heading narrow">
		<p class="eyebrow">Public policy</p>
		<h2>Privacy policy in plain language.</h2>
		<p>This is the public policy reference for Chad: Cycling Coach. It mirrors the product's local-first behavior and keeps the promises concrete.</p>
	</div>

	<div class="policy-card">
		<div class="policy-copy">
			<p>Chad does not require an account to import rides and does not upload imported FIT ride data to a coaching server.</p>
			<p>Ride data imported via FIT files is processed entirely on your device and stored in a local on-device database. Your ride history stays on your device.</p>
			<p>In-app purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase receipt to validate your unlock. No personal information, account data, or ride data is shared with RevenueCat.</p>
